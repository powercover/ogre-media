Publisher privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What this app is

This app posts videos that we made ourselves to our own Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, such as Ogre Reacting. Only the owner of these channels uses it. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it uses

The app uses the access tokens of our own Pages and Instagram accounts, so that it can publish our videos and read their view counts. These tokens are kept on our own computer and are never shared or sold.

The app does not collect, store or share any personal data about other people. It does not read messages, comments or follower lists.

Data deletion

The app keeps no data about anyone except our own accounts. If you still want to ask about data, contact us through the email on our channels' pages, and we will answer and delete anything related to you within 30 days.
